# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are the informal workforce—including artisans, laborers, and service workers—who possess significant skills but lack formal documentation (resumes, certifications) or traditional banking history. They operate in environments where trust is local and reliability is proven through tenure and community reputation.

## Product Purpose

Karigar empowers the informal workforce by bridging the gap between skill and opportunity. It provides a voice-first professional identity and a community-driven financial system, allowing workers to be recognized for their expertise and build a credit-like "Trust Score" without needing formal credentials or traditional bank accounts. Success for the MVP is a Technical Proof-of-Concept demonstrating a seamless voice-to-passport flow and a functional trust scoring mechanism.

## Positioning

Unlike traditional job boards or credit bureaus, Karigar uses AI to structure natural voice interactions into a "Skill Passport" and converts community reliability into a "CT Score" (Community Trust), making trust a verifiable and portable financial asset.

## Operating Context

The product operates in a voice-first paradigm, designed for users who may have low digital literacy or limited access to smartphones (utilizing IVR/voice calls). It integrates with local "Lending Circles"—rotating savings and credit groups—where financial growth is managed through community rituals and peer accountability.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Skill Passport**: AI-powered generation of professional identities from voice conversations.
- **CT Score**: A trust-based credit metric derived from reliability and community tenure.
- **Lending Circles**: Management of community-powered savings and credit groups.
- **Marketplace**: A gallery for the discovery of skilled workers.
- **Multilingual Support**: Must support multiple regional languages to ensure accessibility.
- **Financial Compliance**: Must adhere to local regulations regarding community lending and savings.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tagline**: "Your Skills. Your Credit. Your Future."
- **Identity**: "Modern Heritage"—combining traditional warmth with high-end digital polish.
- **Voice**: Empowering, accessible, and respectful, avoiding technical terminology.

## Evidence on Hand

- **Landing Page**: High-fidelity hero and value proposition sections.
- **Onboarding Flow**: Interactive voice-first intake and passport generation.
- **Marketplace**: Basic artisan discovery and gallery implementation.
- **Assets**: Karigar logo and hero illustration.

## Product Principles

1. **Trust over Documentation**: Reliability and community tenure are treated as primary evidence of expertise, overriding the need for formal resumes.
2. **Voice as the Interface**: Every barrier to entry is removed by making the primary interaction natural, spoken, and intuitive.
3. **Identity as an Asset**: Informal experience is converted into a structured, verified professional identity that opens new doors.
4. **Community-Led Finance**: Financial growth is enabled through local, rotating trust networks rather than centralized institutional banking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Low-Literacy UX**: Interfaces are designed for users with minimal digital experience.
- **Voice-First**: Primary interactions are spoken to ensure inclusivity for non-readers or those without smartphones.
